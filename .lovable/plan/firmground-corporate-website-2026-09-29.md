# FirmGround corporate website

## Goal
Build the complete single-page FirmGround Technologies website from the supplied brief, with a premium light corporate identity focused on clarity, trust, capability, proof, and consultation conversion.

## Brand and visual system
- Use the exact **FirmGround** typography-only wordmark throughout the page.
- Remove visible Lovable/template branding, placeholder metadata, and the default Lovable favicon.
- Create a compact, legible **FG** favicon specifically for browser tabs; no icon or monogram will be used as the on-page logo.
- Apply the supplied navy, blue, green, white, and neutral palette through semantic design tokens.
- Use clean sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, restrained shadows, subtle borders, and balanced motion.
- Build the hero’s technology ecosystem from lightweight interface fragments, connecting lines, and system nodes rather than stock photography.

## Page structure
1. Sticky navigation with desktop links, accessible mobile menu, and consultation action.
2. Hero with the supplied positioning, one primary action, global-service statement, and animated technology ecosystem.
3. Muted trusted-client wordmarks.
4. Eleven service cards using structured data and line icons.
5. Distinct CRMaster and LMS product presentations, preserving the LMS URL as an explicit placeholder.
6. Partnership promise and five FirmGround values.
7. Five-step working process with a restrained animated connector.
8. Six portfolio links opening the supplied client websites in new tabs.
9. Clearly labelled written-testimonial placeholders.
10. Clearly labelled video-feedback placeholders ready for future YouTube links.
11. Startup, creator, and education-business audience section.
12. Three insight placeholders linking toward the external FirmGround blog.
13. Accessible FAQ accordion using the supplied answers.
14. Final consultation callout and validated consultation form.
15. Footer with supplied contact details, external blog link, and clearly disabled/placeholder social and legal links.
16. Persistent WhatsApp and call actions that remain compact on mobile.

## Consultation experience
- Every “Book a Free Consultation” action will smoothly move visitors to the form.
- Validate the required name, company, WhatsApp number, email, and requirement fields.
- On valid submission, construct the exact requested message, URL-encode it, and open WhatsApp for **+91 8329034989**.
- Keep this client-side because the requested flow does not require storing personal data.

## Quality and safeguards
- Organize editable services, products, work, testimonials, videos, insights, and FAQs as structured content.
- Use semantic headings, one H1, keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus states, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion support.
- Optimize each section separately for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile with no horizontal overflow.
- Add homepage SEO metadata and truthful Organization structured data only; do not invent statistics, claims, testimonials, URLs, pricing, or legal copy.
- Keep external portfolio/product destinations explicit and secure, and open only the destinations requested in new tabs.

## Verification
- Confirm the page compiles without errors and contains no remaining template placeholder.
- Test navigation, mobile menu, FAQ, all consultation actions, form validation, WhatsApp message generation, external links, and floating contact actions.
- Visually inspect desktop and mobile layouts for readability, overlap, overflow, and motion behavior.
- Verify the browser tab shows the FG favicon and no user-facing Lovable branding remains.
